Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Aim Phase is published by Zichen Play Studio. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Data We Collect

Aim Phase does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information. The app does not require an account, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not use tracking technologies.

On-Device Data

To operate the game, Aim Phase stores game progress, tutorial completion, music preference, and haptics preference locally on your device using system storage. This information stays on your device and is removed when the app is deleted, subject to your device backup settings.

Children's Privacy

Because Aim Phase does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third Parties

Aim Phase contains no third-party advertising, analytics, payment, social, or tracking services.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Zichen Play Studio through the support contact listed on the app's App Store product page.
